About ListenApp

ListenApp is a private, restricted-access, safeguarding support application made available through approved partner organisations (like domestic abuse charities) who support people who may be at risk of abuse or harm.

The application enables users to discreetly send pre-configured alert messages to trusted contacts using voice triggers or manual activation features.

ListenApp is designed to support users in contacting trusted contacts and emergency services where needed.

What ListenApp is NOT: ListenApp is not an emergency response provider and does not replace police, medical, safeguarding, or professional crisis support services. It does not monitor you – ListenApp does not listen to, watch, or manage emergency situations.

The Emergency 999 button: The application includes a user-activated button enabling the user to initiate a direct call to emergency services (999). ListenApp does not monitor, route, manage, or respond to emergency calls.

Calls made using the Emergency 999 button are placed using your device’s normal telephone functionality. As a result, the call will appear in your device’s call history, recent calls list, and records maintained by your mobile network provider.

Access to ListenApp may require an access code provided by an approved partner organisation.

CRITICAL WARNING: If your phone is off, disconnected, out of battery, has no signal, or has no internet connection, neither the alert messages to your contacts nor the 999 button will work. ListenApp relies on your physical phone and your mobile or internet connectivity to function properly. The application may not function correctly if your device or network is not working.

Voice Triggers, Audio Features, and Device Processing

Voice trigger detection and audio processing operate locally on the user’s device.

ListenApp does not remotely monitor conversations, process live audio streams, or conduct continuous surveillance.

Where users enable audio recording functionality:

  • Recordings are stored within the application environment on the user’s device

When audio recording or background listening functionality is enabled, your device operating system may display visual indicators, including microphone access indicators, recording icons, persistent notifications, or similar system-generated notices. These indicators are controlled by your device operating system and cannot be disabled by ListenApp. Such indicators help ensure the application can continue operating in the background in accordance with operating system requirements.

Messaging and Communications

ListenApp uses third-party communications providers, including Twilio as its primary SMS delivery provider and Infobip as a contingency delivery provider where required, to facilitate SMS alert functionality and related communications services.

When an alert is activated, ListenApp may process:

  • Recipient phone number(s)
  • User-generated alert message content
  • Transmission timestamps
  • Delivery status information
  • Optional location information attached by the user

This processing is limited solely to enabling alert delivery, system reliability, safeguarding functionality, and security monitoring.

Messaging data is retained for a limited period and automatically deleted or made unavailable in accordance with our Privacy Policy and the applicable provider retention controls.

ListenApp relies on third-party communications providers, including Twilio as its primary SMS delivery provider and Infobip as a contingency delivery provider where operationally required. Where Infobip is used, Infobip’s target service availability is 95%. ListenApp cannot guarantee message delivery where outages, delays or service interruptions occur within third-party communications infrastructure.

Messaging providers used by ListenApp may apply transmission controls, anti-spam measures, or rate limits as part of their communications infrastructure. When Infobip is used as the contingency delivery provider, Infobip may restrict the transmission of substantially identical messages sent to the same recipient to six (6) messages per hour and messages containing different content sent to the same recipient to twenty (20) messages per hour. These limits are determined by the communications provider and may change without notice. As a result, some messages may be delayed, restricted, or not delivered where provider-imposed limits are reached.
